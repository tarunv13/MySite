Papers, software, the talks where the ideas were first tested, and the training behind them.

One paper is under external review and one is in preparation. The software is archived on Zenodo, so it can be cited like any other output. The training section says what each course covered and where I used it afterwards, because a list of certificates on its own says very little .

01 · Papers

Papers

  1. 2026

    Preprint · in review at Environment & Behavior

    From knowledge to behaviour change: a mixed-methods evaluation of the Tide Turners Plastic Challenge in India

    Verma, T. K., Bhatia, S., Sinclair, S., Ribeiro, Y., & Veríssimo, D. (2026). SocArXiv. doi:10.31235/osf.io/48gbd_v1

    A randomised pre-post evaluation of UNEP’s youth plastic programme in Delhi and Gujarat, with interviews across six states. First author of five, with co-authors at Oxford, ATREE, Kent and PUC-Rio.

  2. In preparation

    With Dr Diogo Veríssimo

    NFT-based conservation fundraising, evaluated against the Veríssimo and Wan (2019) demand-reduction framework

    Verma, T. K., & Veríssimo, D. Asks whether conservation NFTs change behaviour, and not only how much money they raise, across cases from South Africa, Brazil, Rwanda and Australia.

05 · Training

Training and certificates

Most of these were taken in 2021, in the last months of my M.Sc. and the first months of volunteering on wildlife trade. Each entry says what it covered and where it showed up in my work afterwards.

  1. Apr 2021

    Qualitative Analysis with NVivo

    Lumivero (QSR International) · credential GZJN3WQO9GQFKE

    Covered
    Coding text into themes, organising codes and cases, and running queries across a project in NVivo.
    Used for
    Coding more than 1,000 Protected Area Update reports at IIT Bombay and the Tide Turners interviews at ATREE. It is also why I later built Sift QDA: I knew exactly what a licence like that costs a student.

  2. Apr 2021

    Conservation Project Planning

    National Geographic Society

    Covered
    Planning a conservation project from the threat to the target and on to outcomes that can be measured.
    Used for
    The planning logic I met again in evaluation work, where a programme’s theory of change decides what can be measured and what cannot.

  3. Apr 2021

    Certificate of Appreciation

    Global Initiative on Decent Jobs for Youth

  4. May 2021

    Ending Illegal Wildlife Trade

    ACAMS (Association of Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialists)

    Covered
    Illegal wildlife trade as a financial crime: how the money moves, and how following it helps enforcement.
    Used for
    Volunteering with the Wildlife Crime Control Bureau from June 2021, and later the network view in WildTrace, which follows links between cases rather than single seizures.

  5. May 2021

    Wildlife Naturalist

    Indian Institute of Information Technology, Kota

  6. Jun 2021

    Understanding Illegal Wildlife Trade

    National Geographic Society

    Covered
    What drives the illegal trade in wildlife, the routes it takes, and the responses to it, from enforcement to demand reduction.
    Used for
    Taken the same month I began tracking suspected online trade for the Wildlife Crime Control Bureau, and the grounding for my work at WCS-India in 2022.

  7. Jun 2021

    Scientific Expeditions for Conservation

    National Geographic Society · credential 6586-2424791

    Covered
    Planning fieldwork: permissions, logistics, safety, and collecting data that others can trust.
    Used for
    Multi-state fieldwork for Tide Turners, where permissions and logistics decided whether any data could be collected at all.

  8. Jun 2021
