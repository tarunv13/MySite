Digital traces of human pressure on biodiversity.

Digital records are increasingly proposed as a way of closing gaps in what we know about threats to species. Those records are not neutral. My work asks when a digital trace can be read as evidence of pressure, and when it only shows us where somebody was looking.

M.Sc. Wildlife Science · B.Tech Computer Science · previously ATREE, IIT Bombay, WCS India, Wildlife Crime Control Bureau

Filled cells: observed records. Outlined cells: pressure with no detection effort behind it. The second group is the research problem.

Research

Four questions, one problem

A digital record reflects both the activity it registers and the conditions under which that activity became visible. A seizure reflects trade and enforcement effort. A pathogen record reflects a species' pathogens and how intensively it has been studied. Separating the two is what turns a signal into evidence.

One

Where trade pressure falls — and where nobody is looking

Species-level trade intensity from open trade records and seizure data, combined with species geography, read alongside detection effort so that an absence of records is not mistaken for an absence of trade.

CITES · seizure corpora · IUCN ranges · spatial analysis

Two

What the traded species carry

Species detected in online trade matched to published host–pathogen records, corrected for research effort per taxon, producing a species-level exposure indicator rather than a disease model. Requires One Health collaboration.

VIRION · CLOVER · EID2 · effort correction

Three

How environmental change is narrated across languages

Multilingual discourse on shared conservation topics, classified against a pre-specified codebook, with human coding as the benchmark. Where automated classification fails outside English, that failure is the finding.

multilingual transformers · IndicNLP · human-coded benchmark

Four

What engagement with nature media actually produces

Mixed-methods evaluation of environmental documentaries. The only strand whose outcome measure sits outside the digital record, and therefore the only direct test of what engagement signals correspond to.

pre/post · treatment–control · survey + interview

Listings

Live grants and open calls

Refreshed weekly from CORDIS bulk data and the EU Funding & Tenders Portal. Two layers, and the difference decides how you use them.

Leads are not vacancies. A live grant means someone holds money and plausibly needs people — it is a person worth writing to, not a post to apply for. Calls are openings with real deadlines. Actual PhD vacancies are announced by individual universities on their own boards and no single feed aggregates them honestly; anyone claiming otherwise is selling a scraper. Work the leads layer, because that is where the advantage is.
